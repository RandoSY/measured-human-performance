## Validation boundary

The guide records substantial host-side/package testing, but also explicitly says that the paired R2.1 system must not be described as physically qualified until real cross-build, flash, micro:bit, speaker, matrix, accelerometer, dual-role BLE, stop-path, and no-participant bench qualification are completed on actual hardware.

The guide also explicitly identifies FFT+ as an experimental educational/research instrument, **not a medical device**. Its configured HR limits are external protocol settings, not medical recommendations, and participant request, observed symptoms/condition, operator judgment, and the approved supervising protocol remain authoritative.

No personal participant record is included in this recovery.
